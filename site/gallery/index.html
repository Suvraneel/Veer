Yours

Share one of your own.

From Radlet

In Settings → Loadouts, the share button copies a radlet:// link or saves a .radletloadout file. Skins have Share under Settings → Wheel. Anyone with Radlet can open either.

In this gallery

Add your file to site/gallery in the repository, list it in items.json, and open a pull request. The tests check that every loadout and skin reads cleanly.

Nothing happens?

Opening a link needs a Radlet with sharing. Update from the menu bar (Check for Updates…), or download the latest. You can also download the file and double-click it.
